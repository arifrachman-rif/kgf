### Topics Discussed & Key Points

1. **Commodity Sourcing in Pontianak (West Kalimantan)**
   - Discussion on shipping commodities (uncorled/glondongan vs. shelled/processed).
   - Risks of shelled commodities: mold and weevils (*kutu*), requiring fumigation and vacuuming, with previous complaints from Brunei shipments.
   - Glondongan (whole/unprocessed) option has minimal risks; can be transported via truck/ferry to Jakarta or containers to Surabaya. Minimum volume target: 1 container/puso.
   - Potential commodities: Candlenut (*kemiri*) and pepper (*lada*) from Bengkayang.
   - Trust and fraud issues: Previous experiences of being scammed by local contacts or suppliers in Pontianak, Palu, Bangka, and Lampung when sending money upfront without verified on-site presence.

2. **Commodity Sourcing in Central Sulawesi (Palu / Parigi Moutong) & Lampung**
   - Plans to check sources in Parigi Moutong, Central Sulawesi.
   - Dam-damar (tree resin, *damar mata kucing*) potential from Krui, Lampung, where an enumerator's/facilitator's family owns a farm and has offered supplies.
   - Previous risk of losing funds (approx. 1 billion IDR in Palu) due to collecting goods in small quantities (1 ton) through untrusted local gatherers. Preference to buy directly from established local aggregators/wholesalers if prices are higher for better security.

3. **Logistics and Samples for Jakarta**
   - Bringing candlenut (*kemiri*) samples to Jakarta.
   - Central Sulawesi (Sultang) potential for cloves (*cengkeh*) and nutmeg (*pala*). Samples will be gathered and brought.
   - Clove pricing issues: Export buyers from Dubai and Saudi Arabia requested low prices (below USD 5,000 / ~80 million IDR), which do not match current local market prices (purchased locally at ~110,000 to 128,000 IDR per kg).

### Decisions Made & Rationale

1. **Start with Unprocessed (Glondongan) Candlenut:**
   - *Rationale:* To minimize risks of mold and weevils during transit, and to avoid handling complexities associated with processed nuts.
2. **Target Volume of One Container/Puso:**
   - *Rationale:* To meet viable shipping scale to Jakarta or Surabaya.
3. **Requirement of Trusted On-Site Personnel:**
   - *Rationale:* Past experiences of being defrauded (90% potential risk of getting scammed when sending money upfront to unverified local contacts) dictate that a dedicated internal company representative must be stationed locally in regions like Pontianak or Sulawesi to oversee weighing and payments.
