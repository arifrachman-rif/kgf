The Landscape Challenge

Systemic Threats to Indonesian Agribusiness

Monoculture vulnerability, climate shocks & EU market exclusion risks.

🌊
USD 2–3B Climate Losses
Annual economic losses for Sumatran smallholders due to deforestation-induced flooding, severe droughts, and soil erosion.
📉
Yield Degradation
Ageing tree stocks and monoculture soil exhaustion keep pepper, coffee, and cocoa farmers below living income thresholds.
🛡️
EUDR Compliance Deficit
Without geolocation polygons & verifiable MRV traceability, 5,000+ smallholders risk complete market exclusion from the EU.
Program Objectives

Integrated Solution Architecture

Combining landscape stewardship, green value chains & innovative finance.

1. Green Transformation
Transition 5,000 smallholders from monoculture to resilient agroforestry (Pepper, Coffee, Cocoa) with high-quality planting material.
2. EUDR Traceability
Deploy TAPAK & Traceapp for farm-to-fork polygon mapping, ensuring 100% deforestation-free compliance for EU exporters.
3. Landscape Finance
Establish a Landscape Finance Approach (LFA) mechanism to leverage EU grant funding with private & blended capital.

Digital Technology

TAPAK: Event-Sourced Traceability

Proprietary ERP system built by KGF for real-time EUDR validation.

Key Features of TAPAK
  • ✓ Polygonal GPS mapping for 5,000 smallholder plots.
  • ✓ Event-sourcing graph architecture (Lot & Log tracking).
  • ✓ AI Climate Decision Support (AI-CCDSS).
  • ✓ Integrated AgriMEL app for offline field data collection.
EUDR Readiness Guaranteed

TAPAK links every bag of Black Pepper, Coffee, and Cocoa directly to verified, non-deforested farmer plots, generating instant audit-ready EU compliance certificates.
