# Draft Posting LinkedIn: KGF's Participation in D-8 Halal Expo Indonesia

PT Klumbayan Gold Farm (KGF), representing the Mutiara Nusa Antara Foundation, is proud to have actively participated in the prestigious D-8 Halal Expo Indonesia. Sharing a booth with the Indonesian Muslim Entrepreneurs Community (KPMI), we brought the finest Lampung spices and sustainable agriculture models to the global stage.

We showcased our premium spice products, including Lampung black pepper, Java long pepper, cloves, cubeb, robusta coffee, and processed cocoa (powder & nibs), while engaging in productive business matching sessions with international buyers from Bangladesh, Iran, and other countries.

Our Director, Arif Rachman, discussed the aGROWforests project with the Minister of Finance of Indonesia & Secretary of KNEKS, Mr. Purbaya Yudhi Sadewa, highlighting its Public-Private Partnership (PPP) scheme, partner collaborations, and cocoa processing progress.

We also explored strategic cooperation opportunities with the Koperasi Desa Merah Putih to strengthen our local agricultural supply chain and support farmer empowerment.

A concrete step toward a more sustainable, equitable, and traceable spice industry.

Let's bring Indonesian spices to the world!
