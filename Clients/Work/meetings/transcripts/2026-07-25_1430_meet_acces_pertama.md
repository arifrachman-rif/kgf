## Discussion

### Akses Program Overview and PT Krumbayan Golf Farm Profile
Fikri explained that the Akses Program operates under the Ministry of UMKM to provide business matching, advisement, and advisory follow-up for curated MSMEs over an effective period of 1.5 to 2 months. The program does not offer direct grant funding or direct financing. 

Pak Arif presented PT Krumbayan Golf Farm (KGF), established in 2022 via internal bootstrapping. Operating in Lampung (Pesawaran, Tanggamus, Lampung Barat, Natar, and Bandar Lampung), KGF works with over 1,000 farmers in sustainable pepper, coffee, and cocoa production through agroforestry and nature-based solutions. Major ongoing initiatives include a GIZ Public-Private Partnership consortium extended through late 2027, a 100-ton pepper purchase order with Verstegen featuring an 80 percent down payment commitment, and active engagements with catalytic and impact funds such as ADM Capital, Supernova, and BPDLH.

### Financing History, Requirements, and Targeted Matchmaking
KGF has no existing corporate debt or formal debt history, with Pak Arif noting a clean personal credit history. Past non-bank funding discussions were stalled due to mismatch in scale or high minimum thresholds, such as ADM Capital's requirement of $3 Million to $4.5 Million.

Pak Arif clarified that KGF strictly avoids conventional debt financing and requires Syariah-compliant financing models. While the original proposal requested Rp5 Billion, the immediate operational priority is Rp3 Billion in working capital for purchasing raw materials from farmers and collectors during the harvest season running from June to November. Capex and machinery requirements are largely fulfilled, with remaining capital redirected toward HACCP certification costs for European and Australian buyers and capacity expansion for their cocoa mini-factory.

The team agreed to target specific Syariah financial institutions and funds:
- **Syariah Banks**: BSI, BCA Syariah, Hijra Bank, Maybank Syariah, and CIMB Syariah.
- **Syariah P2P / Crowdfunding**: ALAMI, FundEx, and ShaFiq.
- **Impact & Catalytic Funds**: Teratai, Supernova, Instellar, and Dompet Dhuafa.
