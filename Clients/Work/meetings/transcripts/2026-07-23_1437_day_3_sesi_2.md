## Discussion

### Sustainability and Pitching Strategies
Sustainability must be applied across the entire business process, covering raw materials, processing, distribution, and customer relations. Successful models like Jejak Bumi demonstrate that using recycled materials can secure international orders from markets like Italy, Paris, Swiss, and the UK. When pitching to buyers, presentations must be structured, systematic, and logical. Instead of using standard company profiles, presentations should begin with emotional hooks such as the impact on farmers and transparent profit margins.

### Buyer Requirements and Documentation
European and German buyers enforce strict checks on certifications, chemical usage, standardized emails, compliance, company awards, and export track records. Businesses need to document financial sustainability through contributions to people and profit, training costs, corporate social responsibility, and taxes. Environmental efforts require waste management and composting plans, while production sustainability depends on responsible sourcing, local materials, non-toxic packaging, and reduced plastic waste. Workplace sustainability involves equal employment opportunities, demographic tracking, gender composition, and structured salary scales.

### EU Deforestation Regulation and Supply Chain Traceability
Exporting to the EU demands strict traceability, including legal documents, GPS polygon coordinates of plots, and compliance with deforestation cutoff dates set at December 31, 2020. Satellite tools such as Forest Wars, Sentinel, and Green Class are utilized to overlay land data and run risk assessments. Key challenges include data consistency, AI geolocation errors, regulatory changes, and bureaucratic hurdles with Indonesian forestry authorities. All compliance data must be managed digitally and retained for at least 5 years to support audits.
