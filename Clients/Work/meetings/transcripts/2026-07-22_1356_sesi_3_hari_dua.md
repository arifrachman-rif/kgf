## Executive Summary

Key requirements for European Union Deforestation-free Regulation (EUDR) compliance and Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) sustainability profiling for SMEs were detailed. ## Discussion

### SNI Standardization and HS Code Classification
Participants reviewed the development of Indonesian National Standards (SNI) for lightwood (sengon, jabon) led by the Indonesia Lightwood Association (ILWA), Ministry of Forestry, and Ministry of Industry to distinguish lightwood properties from hardwood. Regarding moringa, a dry leaf SNI exists under the Ministry of Agriculture, while a moringa powder SNI is currently being processed with the Ministry of Industry. Exporters noted an ongoing HS code dispute where Indian moringa exporters register products under medical herbs codes, whereas Indonesian exporters target food or functional standards, causing classification issues in export markets.

### Spices and Agricultural Quality Control Case Studies
Group discussions analyzed post-harvest contamination risks and solutions across agricultural commodities:
- Heavy Metal Contamination (Group 3): Main causes include roadside drying exposed to vehicle exhaust, soil fertilizer contamination, and unsafe water sources. Solutions include relocating drying areas, using solar dome dryers, covering goods with mesh netting, applying organic fertilizers, treating soil with zeolite, and conducting water quality testing.
- Moisture and Mold Management (Group 1): Caused by inadequate post-harvest drying, uninspected supply, and moisture absorption during transport (e.g., open trucks covered with tarps during rain). Solutions include solar dome dryers, silica gel moisture absorbers, and dry wooden pallets. Re-drying moldy goods was highlighted as a risk to essential oil content and flavor profile.
- Aflatoxin and Mold in Nutmeg and Pepper (Group 2): Caused by unventilated humid storage, direct floor contact, and poor airtight packaging. Solutions include controlled packaging temperature, hygrometer installation, FIFO/FEFO inventory systems, ventilated facilities, and replacing raw wooden pallets with oven-dried wooden or plastic pallets.

### Seaweed Quality and Food Safety Management
Seaweed moisture levels directly determine carrageenan extraction rates and gel strength quality. The Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries (KKP) requires purchasing seaweed from certified CPIB (Good Fish Farming Practice) sources, though adoption among smallholder farmers remains low due to certification costs and tax concerns. Biological hazards such as mold and Salmonella are mitigated using steam sterilization, controlled chlorine washing (maintained below the Ministry of Health limit of 5 ppm), and activated carbon filtration.

### GRI Standards and SME Sustainability Reporting
A pitch deck template based on Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) standards was presented to help SMEs build sustainability profiles. These profiles will be promoted to Indonesian Trade Promotion Center (ITPC) offices in Japan, Europe, and the USA to give priority to sustainable exporters. The reporting framework emphasizes tracking energy reduction and implementing circular economy practices, such as converting sawdust wood waste into oven fuel and briquettes.

### EUDR Compliance and Supply Chain Traceability
The European Union Deforestation-free Regulation (EUDR) has transitioned from a voluntary framework to a mandatory compliance requirement covering wood, cocoa, palm oil, coffee, rubber, and soya. Land utilized prior to the December 2020 cut-off date is exempt from deforestation claims unless land degradation occurred post-December 2020. Exporters must provide land legal status, GeoJSON polygon coordinate mapping for satellite verification, and Due Diligence Statements (DDS). SVLK is being upgraded to SVLK Plus for wood traceability, while STDB registration is being evaluated for coffee and cocoa alongside non-wood forest products (bamboo, resin, sago, honey, essential oils). DDS submissions are required only once if the supply plot, source, and commodity remain unchanged across shipments.

### Funding Opportunities and Industry Events
The Swiss Sustainable Coffee Platform is offering grants up to SFr 100,000 (approximately IDR 1.8 billion) for coffee and cocoa knowledge exchange programs and workshops. Additionally, participants were invited to the upcoming National Wood Conference scheduled for July 28 or 29 in Semarang.
