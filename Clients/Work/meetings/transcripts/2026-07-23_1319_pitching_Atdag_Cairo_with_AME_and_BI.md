## Executive Summary
The meeting addressed updates on coffee harvesting, export logistics for small green bean shipments, and presentation guidelines for Bank Indonesia (BI) events. Due to urgent KBRI Cairo preparations for Independence Day, the formal pitching session with AtDag Kairo was postponed to next week. Additionally, AtDag Kairo invited BI Lampung SMEs to participate in the Food Africa Expo in Cairo this December, with a plan to curate seven companies and seek co-funding from BI.

---

## Discussion

### Coffee Harvest Updates and Export Logistics
Bu Galkov provided an update on the Lampung coffee harvest and sample shipments sent to London via BI. Pak Reza noted significant domestic coffee market movements, including Java Coffee Flavor Festival (JFF) transactions reaching IDR 68.4 billion and a 30% to 50% drop in harvest yields that sharply increased green bean prices. Regarding export logistics for 100 to 200 kg of coffee beans, Pak Reza advised Bu Galkov to export independently via LCL (Less than Container Load) rather than using undername services or EMS, leveraging her existing Business Identification Number (NIB).

### SME Preparation for Bank Indonesia Events
Anyo shared that partnership arrangements with local farmer groups are underway to secure mushroom raw material supply for anticipated bulk orders from BI initiatives. Pak Reza emphasized that BI frequently orders goodie bag items from partner SMEs. He instructed all participating SMEs to trim their pitching presentations, omitting lengthy vision and mission statements to focus concisely on company overviews, products, export history, production capacity, and target markets.

### Rescheduling Pitching Session with AtDag Kairo
Bu Ovi (AtDag Kairo) requested to reschedule the pitching session due to conflicting commitments, including preparations for the August 17 Independence Day events at KBRI Cairo and official delegation meetings. The group agreed to hold the rescheduled session next week. The pitching format for the 9 BI Lampung SMEs will consist of a 3-minute presentation and a 2-minute Q&A per SME, alongside a brief overview from AtDag Kairo, keeping the total session under one hour.

### Collaboration for Food Africa Expo in Cairo
Bu Ovi invited the BI Lampung SMEs to participate in the Food Africa Expo in Cairo in December. She plans to curate 7 companies out of the 9 SMEs, provide market preparation support, and lead business matching efforts. Because AtDag Kairo's trade budget is currently locked in Jakarta, Bu Ovi requested BI co-funding support. Pak Reza confirmed he will submit a sponsorship proposal to BI, citing previous successful expo collaborations.
