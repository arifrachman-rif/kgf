## Discussion

### Blunomi Company and Project Overview
Sophia and Neil introduced Blunomi as a consulting firm based in France with regional offices, focusing exclusively on climate transition, energy, and natural capital. Blunomi is currently supporting a European Development Finance Institution, which is the French equivalent of GIZ, to deploy capital for agricultural debt financing in Southeast Asia using a regenerative agriculture framework.

### PT Kalam Bayan Konkram and Fairfood Project Overview
Arif and Payu presented PT Kalam Bayan Konkram, established in 2022 in Lampung, which manages an agroforestry consortium project across three subdistricts with 1,300 farmers. The project is 70 percent grant funded by GIZ under a public private partnership, involving commercial partner First Stegen and NGO Fairfood. The operations focus on black pepper, white pepper, coffee, and cacao. The team outlined plans to scale downstream activities for cacao, train farmers in bean fermentation, and implement digital blockchain mapping for EUDR readiness. Current revenue is generated through black pepper exports to First Stegen, with targets to expand capacity and reach 20 containers per year by 2030.

### Financing Needs and Constraints
The project partners noted that local smallholders and PT Kalam Bayan Konkram face significant bottlenecks in accessing financial assistance. Capital is urgently needed to build centralized processing and fermentation facilities for cacao and spices, enabling quick cash purchases from smallholders to bypass local middlemen. Furthermore, the company lacks traditional collateral for debt financing since their land assets cannot be used for this purpose.
